About Ugaverse

Experience heritage. Visit new places. Discover art & culture.

We started Ugaverse to help people explore Ugandan places and stories from wherever they are.

Today, we build immersive projects for businesses and organisations, while finding new ways to share the best of Uganda with the world.

From our Buganda Palace project. → Daddy's House, a creative homestay in Naalya. → Timeless Frames, a virtual photo exhibition. →

Our work is defined by 3 rules.

  1. 01

    Context

    It must be tailored to the local context.

    Our Buganda Palace tour comes from a real visit to the palace in Kampala.

  2. 02

    Quality

    It must be the best quality we can make.

    We visit, measure and scan each place, then build it as a 3D model.

  3. 03

    Accessibility

    It must be easy for people to open and use.

    The Buganda Palace tour opens on a phone, tablet or computer.
